# Code of conduct

Bothy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good faith, and focus on what is best for the
project and its users. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on <https://github.com/bothy-sync/bothy> (it reaches only
the maintainers), or by opening an issue asking a maintainer to contact you. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments and block people who break these rules.
